What it is for

In NBA 2K27 a build costs VC and cannot be undone. Before you commit, three things matter: how high each attribute can go with the height, weight and wingspan you picked; which badges those attributes unlock, and at which tier; which animations will stay out of reach. The game answers none of this until the build already exists.

This site answers while you move the sliders, and shows what one more point would win you — or cost you elsewhere.

Where to start

The builder Pick your body, spend your attribute points, and watch the maximums, badges and animations move as you go. All 53 badges What each badge does, the attributes needed for Bronze, Silver, Gold and Hall of Fame, and the height required. The animations 2,595 animations by category, from the easiest to unlock to the most demanding, with the attributes each one asks for. Community builds Builds published by players, and 3,679 real builds to compare yours with what people actually play. My build Your full sheet: badges and their tiers, Cap Breaker plan, progress to follow in game. The trios Ready-to-play templates if you don't know where to start: pick one and adjust it afterwards.

Where the numbers come from

Attribute maximums are read by hand from the in-game builder and the NBA 2K HQ app, across 340 bodies. Animation requirements are cross-checked between public sources, and the site always says whether a value is exacte, estimated ou approximative — rather than showing everything in the same voice.

This site is an independent tool made by a player. It is not affiliated with 2K or the NBA, and reproduces no artwork from the game.
