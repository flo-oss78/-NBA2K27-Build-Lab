What this site does with your data

The site asks for no account, no sign-up and no password.

  • Your build stays with you. It is saved in your browser's own storage. It is not sent anywhere unless you publish it.
  • Published build. If you use “Publish”, the build (body, attributes, name, description) becomes visible to everyone and gets a public address. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time by e-mail.
  • Abuse protection. Each time you publish or like something, the site stores a hashed fingerprint of your IP address to limit how many requests you can send per minute. That fingerprint cannot be turned back into your IP address, and it is deleted after about an hour.
  • Comments. They are closed. Comments written before they closed are still visible; write to us to have one removed.
  • Advertising. The site shows no advertising to date, and therefore sets no advertising trackers: that is why you see no cookie banner. Should it ever show ads, this page would say so, and your consent would be requested before any tracker is set. Without your consent, no advertising tracker is set.
  • No resale. No data is sold or rented, and none is passed to a third party for advertising purposes without your prior consent.

You can ask to access, correct or delete anything that concerns you by writing to contact@lelabodesbuilds.com.

Where the game data comes from

  • Personal recordings: the attribute maximums for each body are recorded in the game's builder and in the NBA 2K HQ app, then checked.
  • Real builds: complete builds published by LockerCodes.
  • Official 2K blueprints (Signature Blueprints): recorded via NBA2KLab.

Sources are credited and linked on the relevant pages. Any rights holder who wants their data removed can write to contact@lelabodesbuilds.com : removal is carried out without discussion.

How reliable the information is

Some of the values shown are estimated from the recorded bodies, and the site says so every time. The game can also change its rules with any update. This site therefore does not guarantee the accuracy of its information and does not replace the game.
